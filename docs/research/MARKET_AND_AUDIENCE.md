# Market and audience notes

Status: **Exploratory research extracted from the historical transcript; time-sensitive facts are not revalidated requirements.**

The transcript argues that the overlap of procedural worlds, sandbox creation, action-RPG progression, exploration, and co-op serves a large audience. It cites Minecraft, Valheim, Enshrouded, No Man's Sky, Hytale, Vintage Story, Trove, Portal Knights, Cube World, and Veloren. Sales/review counts and release status were observations at the time of that conversation and must be refreshed before business use.

## Behavioral audience segments

| Segment | Retention drivers |
|---|---|
| Explorer | Rare geography, landmarks, caves, ruins, creatures, secrets, causal history |
| Action-RPG adventurer | Responsive combat, builds, abilities, equipment, bosses, readable challenge |
| Builder | Persistent transformation, bases, villages, roads, useful structures |
| Systems player | Crafting, ecology, economy, factions, optimization, explainable consequence |
| Social player | Cooperative expeditions, complementary roles, trade, shared discovery and construction |

These are design lenses, not mutually exclusive personas or validated market sizes.

## Research hypotheses

- Size and seed count do not create retention without purpose and meaningful variation.
- Visible capability growth and process changes are stronger than repeated numerical tiers.
- Travel friction and weak combat can undermine excellent world generation.
- Creation, multiplayer, mods, and systemic stories provide human-generated longevity.
- A procedural-first asset pipeline may be a strong production and technical differentiator, but only if output quality is demonstrable.

## Required future research

- Revalidate market size, comparable performance, pricing, platforms, and current community sentiment from primary/store data.
- Conduct player interviews/playtests rather than treating Reddit/Steam anecdotes as representative samples.
- Test the elevator pitch and audiovisual proposition without leading respondents.
- Separate creator production constraints from player-valued product features.
