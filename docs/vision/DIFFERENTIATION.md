# Differentiation

Status: **Accepted product direction**.

## Defining combination

Veldwake combines two uncommon commitments:

1. a persistent procedural action-RPG world with causal memory at multiple simulation resolutions;
2. a coherent audiovisual identity compiled from descriptors, constraints, grammars, geometry, shaders, procedural animation, DSP, and controlled musical systems.

Neither part is sufficient alone. A living world without satisfying movement and combat is a simulation demo. Procedural art without strong style rules is content noise. A beautiful endless map without consequence is scenery.

## What “own identity” requires

- geography with geological/hydrological reasons, not noise alone;
- places explained by resources, cultures, routes, and history;
- equipment with semantic origin, geometry, use, and history—not adjective/stat spam;
- generated cultures with consistent architecture, palette, material, and silhouette grammar;
- traversal that develops into gameplay rather than dead time;
- combat validated early for readability, response, audio, VFX, animation, and camera feel;
- inspection tools that expose causal state and generator decisions.

## Reference versus requirement

| Reference | Inspiration / problem addressed | Do not copy / failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Cube World | Adventure, recognizable voxel landscapes, movement, action-RPG exploration | Identity, regional progression that invalidates rewards, or content structure wholesale |
| Veloren | Rust feasibility, large landscapes, atmosphere, client/server separation, aggregate world simulation | Weak impact/readability in combat, repetitive interaction, or travel friction |
| Minecraft | A world made of editable matter and player-created longevity | Cube/texture-atlas identity, progression, or being “Minecraft with shaders” |
| Vintage Story | Visible mastery, deep material progression, meaningful processes | Tedious travel or systems whose time cost exceeds their decisions |
| Valheim | Expedition, preparation, co-op, readable progression, strong atmosphere with simple assets | Copying biome/boss progression or survival pacing |
| No Man's Sky | Procedural scale and long-term systemic expansion | Treating variation or scale as content by itself |
| Dwarf Fortress | History, civilizations, causal simulation, stories | Microscopic simulation without player-visible consequences |
| Zelda | Discovery, traversal growth, environmental interaction | Specific characters, mechanics, visual language, or encounter design |
| Hytale, Terraria, Trove, Portal Knights, Enshrouded | Sandbox/RPG audience overlap, creation, accessibility, builds, co-op | Generic feature accumulation, purposeless worlds, repetitive grind, or time gates |
