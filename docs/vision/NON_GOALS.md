# Non-goals

Status: **Accepted for the current horizon** unless superseded by an ADR and roadmap change.

- Photorealism or a conventional AAA asset pipeline.
- Routine dependence on Blender, hand-authored models/textures/pixel art, large recorded SFX libraries, or manually composed soundtrack content.
- Unity, Unreal, Godot, Bevy, or an equivalent ready-made central game engine.
- Copying Cube World, Veloren, Minecraft, or any other reference.
- An MMORPG or globally persistent service in the first product slice.
- Simulating every NPC physically at all times or every microscopic detail.
- Building solved infrastructure—OS drivers, complete physics engine, swapchain abstraction—from pride rather than product value.
- Supporting every desktop/console/mobile platform in the first prototype.
- Creating every future crate, subsystem, plugin API, or optimization during foundation work.
- Infinite terrain as a substitute for authored rules, progression, encounters, and consequences.
- Bitwise determinism for every simulation operation on every hardware target.
- Fast travel as an automatic early fix for uninteresting travel; traversal is a design system first.
- Committing to every candidate library before its capability is scheduled and evaluated.
