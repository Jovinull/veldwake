# Game vision

Status: **Accepted direction**, with individual features staged by roadmap.

Veldwake is a 3D voxel action RPG about exploration in a large, finite, seed-generated, persistent fantasy world. Geography, ecology, creatures, settlements, factions, trade, and events may evolve at different simulation resolutions. The player explores, fights, learns, travels, builds, and alters the world; important consequences remain observable later.

A second, equally important proposition is production through systems. Geometry, materials, creatures, equipment, vegetation, animation, VFX, ambience, sound effects, and music should be predominantly generated or synthesized by code under explicit art direction. This is not permission for uncurated randomness or low quality.

## Player fantasy

The player is an increasingly capable wanderer in a coherent world that existed before arrival and does not reduce to a theme-park checklist. A distant landmark should invite an expedition; gained knowledge and traversal should turn old barriers into new routes; and interventions should leave legible history.

The fantasy supports several compatible behaviors:

- explorers seek rare geography, ruins, creatures, caves, and causal stories;
- action-RPG players seek readable, satisfying combat and expressive builds;
- builders leave useful, visible marks through camps, structures, roads, and settlements;
- systems players understand and influence ecology, resources, trade, and factions;
- social players cooperate, specialize, trade, build, and discover together.

## Desired forms of longevity

Longevity is not equivalent to a numerically enormous seed space. The intended sources are:

- spatial: geography, caves, regions, ruins, and routes;
- combinatorial: creatures, equipment, encounters, builds, and generated assets;
- systemic: populations, resources, danger, trade, factions, and world memory;
- human: construction, cooperative play, mods, challenges, and shared stories.

Meaningful exploration, mastery, combat, construction, world alteration, simulation, multiplayer, and modding should reinforce one another. Infinite terrain full of interchangeable content is explicitly insufficient.

## Product boundary

References such as Cube World and Veloren describe desired feelings or useful lessons, not specifications. Veldwake must establish its own visual grammar, progression, causal world model, and combat feel. The target is a high-quality stylized voxel fantasy—not a Minecraft reskin, a Cube World clone, or a technical simulation without a compelling game.
